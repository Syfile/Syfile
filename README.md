# Syfile — Official Releases

**Syfile** is a local-first file optimizer, organizer, and `.sybackup` packager for desktop and mobile. Your files are processed **100% locally on your device** — never uploaded to any cloud.

## Key Features

- **Storage Optimization Without Deleting Files:** Detects duplicate content and stores repeated data once while keeping every file accessible and restorable to its exact original state.
- **Automatic Local Organization:** Organizes Photos, Videos, Music, and Documents cleanly on your own disk.
- **Instant Streaming & Faithful Restoration:** Open files directly from their optimized state or restore originals anytime with `.sybackup` backups.
- **100% Private & Local:** No cloud storage of your library; works offline once your account license is synced.
